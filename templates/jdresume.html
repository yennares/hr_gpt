Job Description and Resume Comparision Page

Introducing a powerful feature that revolutionizes the way hiring managers find the right candidates for their job openings. Our feature takes the job description and the resume of any employee as inputs and provides a matching score in terms of percentage, along with a brief summary that hiring managers can easily understand and use to make informed decisions.

The matching score is presented as a percentage, with higher percentages indicating a better match between the job description and the candidate's resume. In addition to the matching score, our feature also provides a brief summary that highlights the key similarities and differences between the job description and the candidate's resume.

With our feature, hiring managers can save time and effort while finding the best candidates for their job openings.
